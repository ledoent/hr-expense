To use this module, you need to:

1.  Go to *Expenses* and create a new *Expense* paid by *Employee (to
    reimburse)*
2.  Submit and approve it, then click on *Post Journal Entries*
3.  Click on *Register Payment*, fill in the wizard and validate: the
    expense's journal entry is now reconciled with a payment
4.  Click on *Reset to Draft*
5.  The expense goes back to *Draft*; the payment is cancelled and the
    journal entry deleted

If the payment was unreconciled from the expense beforehand, the reset still
returns the expense to *Draft* but leaves that payment *In Process* for the
accountant to settle. Its *Cancel* button is hidden in that state, so reset
the payment to draft first if you want to cancel it.

With expense reports installed, resetting a report removes all of its journal
entries together. A single expense of a report that has several entries cannot
be reset on its own.
